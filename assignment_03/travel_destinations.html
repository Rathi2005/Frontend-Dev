Top 3 Travel Destinations

1. Paris, France

Paris is famed for its culture, food, and iconic landmarks such as the Eiffel Tower.

"A walk about Paris will provide lessons in history, beauty, and in the point of life." — Thomas Jefferson

2. Kyoto, Japan

Kyoto is known for its temples, gardens, and preserved traditional culture.

"Traveling — it leaves you speechless, then turns you into a storyteller." — Ibn Battuta

3. Goa, India

Goa offers beautiful beaches, lively markets, and a relaxed coastal vibe.

"To travel is to discover that everyone is wrong about other countries." — Aldous Huxley

Travel Tips

  • Carry photocopies of important documents.
  • Learn basic local phrases.
  • Keep emergency contacts handy.
